# Roblox 3D Asset Workflow

## Choose the simplest path

1. Roblox-native Parts/MeshParts for simple stylized geometry.
2. Direct Blender modeling for hard-surface or controlled hero assets.
3. Image-to-3D generation when it genuinely saves time on organic/complex forms.

Do not route every asset through AI 3D generation.

## Generated-asset pipeline

1. Define asset role, dimensions, silhouette, palette, and reference constraints.
2. Generate/choose a concept image if needed.
3. Review the concept before spending 3D-generation compute.
4. Generate the mesh.
5. Render/check several angles.
6. Clean topology and reduce complexity to a Roblox-appropriate budget.
7. Bake/retain detail through textures where useful.
8. Verify scale, pivot, collisions, materials, and texture use.
9. Upload/import through the approved Roblox pipeline when configured.
10. Inspect the asset inside Studio at gameplay camera distance.

## Rights

Track the source/license for generated models, source images, textures, and external assets. Do not assume a model or asset pack is commercially usable.
