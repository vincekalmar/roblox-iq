# Open Design Questions

These are intentionally unresolved. Do not silently convert a brainstorm into a permanent system.

## Money generation

How exactly does a visualized item generate money?

Directions discussed:
- sell/value the vivid mental image
- rewards based on how difficult/large/strange the item is
- a more direct automatic reward loop

Current constraint: the starting area should not require a traditional general shop building just to perform routine progression.

## Visualization interaction

What is the actual skill/input used to comprehend an object?

A simple repeated-click prototype is acceptable for testing, but the final interaction should ideally support the mental-visualization fantasy better.

## Spinning / mental manipulation

How active should "spinning" an item be?

Questions:
- continuous player input or mostly passive training?
- does item complexity affect training behavior?
- how does the study station support/amplify it?

## Movement progression

IQ should not simply make running speed arbitrarily high unless testing proves that feels best.

Promising directions:
- IQ-gated bicycles/hoverboards/advanced gear
- shortcuts that become understandable at higher IQ
- teleportation or conceptual travel later
- plot/world technology that reduces traversal friction

No final system is selected.

## Research Center

Current role: a plot structure purchased after early progression and a likely home for deeper upgrades/research.

Still undecided:
- which upgrades live there
- whether it unlocks systems, improves objects, or both
- whether upgrades are purchased with money, IQ requirements, research points, or a combination

Do not invent a new research currency unless there is a clear design reason.

## Item modifiers

Earlier ideas include bigger/crazier/mutated items being more valuable or difficult to visualize.

Open question: whether this becomes rarity, mutations, size modifiers, area-specific variants, or is omitted entirely.

## Prestige / rebirth

Not designed yet. Do not implement one merely because the genre often uses rebirths.

## Monetization

Not designed yet. Keep core progression architecture independent from assumed gamepass/dev-product behavior.

## UI art direction

General readability rules are documented, but the final typography, component language, colors, and icon style should be established from approved references before Codex mass-produces UI.
