# World Structure

## Purpose

World design in Roblox IQ should make progression legible before the player reads a menu.

Every area should answer three questions quickly:

1. What can I interact with here?
2. Where is my progression home / route back to my plot?
3. What can I see that I am not smart enough to access yet?

## Starting structure

The current world begins with a compact floating Apple Orchard island containing:
- spawn
- central orchard / first visualization targets
- six player plots around the shared activity
- a visible next-area comprehension gate

See `STARTING-ISLAND.md`.

## Area philosophy

New areas are not only higher-number zones. They represent increasingly difficult parts of the world the player has learned to comprehend.

Each area should ideally provide:
- a distinct visual theme
- its own family of visualizable items
- a clear difficulty increase
- at least one visible aspirational target
- a route/transition to the next progression stage

## Area size

Keep areas compact until traversal upgrades justify larger spaces.

Walking time should support gameplay rather than act as filler.

As mobility improves, later areas may become broader or more vertically interesting.

## Shared world vs plots

The core rhythm depends on quick movement between:
- shared area content
- the player's personal plot

Do not place plots so far away that routine training/upgrading becomes a commute.

Future areas do not necessarily need new plots. The player's plot can remain a persistent home while areas are reached through comprehension gates/portals/transport.

## Environmental progression

A strong future direction is allowing high IQ to change how older environments are perceived.

Examples:
- hidden shortcuts become understandable
- previously meaningless structures become usable
- advanced interactables reveal themselves
- new objects become comprehensible in old areas

This is not required for the first prototype but fits the theme strongly.

## Landmark hierarchy

Use a small number of strong landmarks rather than many competing structures.

Typical hierarchy:
1. current gameplay center
2. personal progression route/plot
3. next aspiration/gate

## Transitions

The current world can use portals/doors/gates between major areas.

The transition should be themed as comprehension rather than generic teleportation where possible.

Do not make every portal enormous or neon. The gate should fit the area's scale and art direction.

## Area document template

When a new area is designed, document:
- purpose in progression
- object family
- IQ range/role (not necessarily final values)
- approximate footprint
- key landmarks
- spawn/entry sightline
- path/traversal structure
- future gate/exit
- canonical assets
- what is intentionally not being built yet
- visual QA viewpoints
- acceptance criteria

## Current area roadmap

### Area 1 — Apple Orchard

**Defined.** See `STARTING-ISLAND.md`.

### Area 2+

**OPEN.** Do not commit to a theme merely to fill a roadmap.

Possible broad escalation directions discussed include study/learning spaces, machines/workshops, city-scale objects, laboratories/advanced technology, cosmic environments, and eventually abstract/conceptual spaces.

These are inspiration, not a locked sequence.
