# VFX Style

Status: **CURRENT DIRECTION / TO BE TRAINED FROM APPROVED EXAMPLES**

## Purpose

VFX should make progression feel increasingly powerful without making routine actions visually exhausting.

## Current rules

- preserve an intensity ladder; not every action deserves maximum spectacle
- favor readable silhouettes and clear effect timing over particle quantity
- use anticipation -> impact -> resolve for important beats
- gameplay targets must remain readable through effects
- scale, sound, camera, UI motion, and lighting can work together; particles do not need to carry everything
- major comprehension events should become increasingly reality-bending as item complexity grows

## Working intensity ladder

1. hover/interaction cue — minimal
2. routine successful action — light
3. successful visualization — satisfying
4. new/rare important item — strong reveal
5. plot upgrade — construction/transformation beat
6. area unlock — major spectacle
7. late-game cosmic/conceptual comprehension — extreme, but coherent

## Library direction

Build named reusable primitives/recipes from project-created or legally usable sources. Examples may include:
- burst
- ring
- radial lines
- glow
- beam
- trail
- spark
- mental wisp
- shockwave
- distortion proxy
- construction flash

Do not lock colors, textures, or recipes until approved Roblox IQ examples exist.
