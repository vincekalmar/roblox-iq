# Game Vision

## Status

This document describes the current intended identity of Roblox IQ. The central fantasy and design pillars are **LOCKED** unless the project deliberately changes direction. Specific mechanics and balance examples are **CURRENT DIRECTION** unless otherwise stated.

## One-sentence fantasy

Start by struggling to mentally visualize something as simple as an apple, then grow your IQ until you can vividly comprehend enormous, bizarre, technological, cosmic, and eventually reality-bending objects and concepts.

## Elevator pitch

Roblox IQ is a simulator/tycoon-style progression game where intelligence is treated as an ability, not just a number. Players explore areas to find increasingly difficult objects, attempt to **visualize/comprehend** them, then mentally manipulate or **spin** those acquired images to train IQ. Money is used to physically improve a personal plot—especially a central Study Station that evolves from a simple desk and book into increasingly advanced cognition technology. Higher IQ unlocks harder items, better tools, new areas, and eventually smarter ways to move through the world.

## Player fantasy

The player is becoming impossibly intelligent.

At the beginning, an apple should feel like a legitimate mental challenge. The player learns to focus on it, successfully hold it in their mind, and train by rotating/manipulating that image.

Over time, the scale of what the player can comprehend should become funny and aspirational:

```text
simple everyday object
→ more complex object
→ machines / structures
→ huge or strange objects
→ advanced technology
→ cosmic objects
→ impossible / conceptual things
```

The emotional arc is: **"I could barely picture an apple; now my brain can comprehend things that should be impossible."**

## Core resources

### IQ

IQ is the player's primary capability stat.

It should matter mechanically by affecting access to:
- harder visualizable items
- later areas
- more advanced plot/study technology
- future movement/transportation technology or shortcuts
- potentially visualization difficulty/success

IQ should generally feel like accumulated capability, not like a currency that is constantly spent down.

### Money

Money is the main spendable progression resource.

Its primary purpose is to improve the player's physical progression infrastructure:
- Study Station upgrades
- Research Center construction/upgrades
- future authored plot structures
- potentially movement/support technology

**Current best-fit design:** successful or high-quality visualization creates economic value, while studying/spinning the resulting mental image primarily trains IQ. Exact payout rules are still open.

## Why this game should exist

The game uses a highly understandable Roblox progression structure but gives it a theme that can justify increasingly ridiculous escalation.

The useful familiar structure is:

```text
shared world content
→ obtain better progression object
→ return/use personal progression setup
→ become stronger
→ visibly upgrade base
→ access better content
```

But the fantasy is specific to Roblox IQ:
- stronger objects require more intelligence to comprehend
- areas can literally be things the player cannot understand yet
- the treadmill-equivalent is a Study Station
- plot progression looks like intellectual/technological development
- movement progression can become smarter technology rather than arbitrary running speed
- endgame content can escalate naturally into absurd concepts

## Target player experience

### First impression

Within seconds of spawning, the player should understand:
1. the apple orchard ahead is where the first gameplay happens;
2. nearby plots belong to players and visibly upgrade;
3. a mysterious/gated route beyond the orchard represents future progression.

### Session feel

- fast early feedback
- compact traversal
- frequent visible upgrades
- repeated short goals rather than long dead periods
- enough active interaction that the game does not feel fully idle
- enough persistent progression that returning to the plot feels satisfying

### Social structure

The game is primarily individual progression inside a shared server.

Social value comes from:
- seeing other players' plot/study-station tiers
- seeing players access areas/items you cannot yet comprehend
- visible advanced technology/items
- potential future cooperative/social systems, but direct PvP is not a current pillar

### Platform priorities

PC and mobile first.

Core actions must not depend on mouse hover, tiny hit targets, or precision controls that fail on touch.

## Design pillars

### 1. Intelligence must change what the player can do

IQ cannot only be a leaderboard number. It should unlock capability, content, technology, or understanding.

### 2. The world should visibly react to progression

Better plot technology, unlocked areas, new traversal methods, and harder targets should make growth visible without opening a stats menu.

### 3. The escalation is the reward

Players should constantly wonder: **"What will I be able to comprehend next?"**

The content itself should become increasingly surprising.

### 4. Familiar structure, distinct fantasy

Use successful Roblox simulator/tycoon readability when it helps, but do not copy another game's surface theme or exact mechanics.

### 5. The plot is a progression display, not a construction sandbox

Plots use authored predetermined upgrade locations so progression remains readable and visually controlled.

### 6. Travel time must earn its place

Early traversal should be compact. If the world becomes larger later, the player should also gain smarter movement, shortcuts, or transportation.

### 7. Avoid unnecessary currencies and systems

Do not add research points, gems, rebirth tokens, pets, rarity systems, or other genre conventions merely because similar games use them. Add a system only when it creates a useful decision or stronger fantasy.

## Tone

The game should take its own progression seriously enough to be satisfying, while allowing the object escalation to become playful and ridiculous.

The humor should mostly come from contrast:
- early player: struggling to visualize an apple
- late player: casually rotating something absurd in their mind

Avoid turning the whole experience into random meme clutter that destroys the progression fantasy.

## Explicit non-goals

- generic pet simulator as the main loop
- unrestricted free-build tycoon
- giant spawn lobby full of disconnected shops
- deep menu navigation for routine upgrades
- hyper-realistic visual style
- giant empty maps used only to increase playtime
- arbitrary speed increases justified only by IQ
- automatic implementation of prestige/rebirth because "simulators need rebirths"
- many currencies with overlapping purposes

## Working title

`Roblox IQ` / `IQ Game` are working names only. Final branding is open.
