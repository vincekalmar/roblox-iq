# Game Vision

## One-sentence fantasy

Start by struggling to mentally visualize something as simple as an apple, then grow your IQ until you can vividly comprehend enormous, impossible, and eventually reality-bending objects and concepts.

## Player fantasy

The player is training their mind.

They discover objects in increasingly advanced areas, attempt to comprehend/visualize them, bring that progress back to a personal plot, and improve the tools that help them train faster.

The satisfying transformation is not only a larger number. The player should visibly progress from a basic study setup and simple objects to advanced technology, research facilities, stranger areas, and more absurd things to comprehend.

## Why this game should exist

The familiar Roblox simulator/tycoon progression structure is wrapped around a different fantasy: intelligence and visualization.

The theme should justify the mechanics rather than merely rename them:
- stronger items require more IQ to comprehend
- areas can be locked because the player cannot yet understand/access them
- the plot's main upgrade path is a study setup rather than a treadmill or generic machine
- movement improvements should eventually feel like smarter technology, shortcuts, or comprehension rather than arbitrary raw speed
- later objects can escalate from everyday things into concepts that would be ridiculous or impossible to visualize in real life

## Target experience

- Session style: fast, readable progression with reasons to return to the plot frequently
- Social/solo: primarily solo progression inside a shared social server
- Competition: indirect/social comparison through visible plot progression and player advancement; direct PvP is not currently a pillar
- Intended pacing: the first useful interaction should happen almost immediately; early upgrades should arrive quickly enough to teach the loop
- Platform priorities: Roblox PC and mobile first; controls/UI should not assume mouse-only play

## Design pillars

1. **IQ must matter mechanically.** Higher IQ should unlock stronger objects, areas, tools, or forms of navigation/comprehension.
2. **Progress should be visible in the world.** A player's plot and study setup should physically evolve rather than progression living only in menus.
3. **The escalation should be fun to anticipate.** Apples and books should eventually give way to huge, strange, mutated, technological, cosmic, or conceptual targets.
4. **Familiar structure, distinct fantasy.** Use proven simulator/tycoon readability without copying another game's identity.
5. **Keep traversal compact until traversal itself becomes progression.** Early areas should not waste the player's time with long empty walks.

## Explicit non-goals

- A generic free-build tycoon
- A giant starting lobby with disconnected shops and menus
- A traditional pet-collection game as the central loop
- Hyper-realistic environment art
- A huge empty world whose only purpose is travel time
- Making every progression decision through abstract menus when it could be shown physically on the plot/world

## Current working title

`Roblox IQ` / `IQ Game` are working names only. Final title is undecided.
