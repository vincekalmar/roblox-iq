# Area Progression

## Status

**CURRENT DIRECTION.** The starting area and comprehension-gate concept are strong. Later area themes/counts are intentionally open.

## Purpose of areas

Areas are not just scenery swaps. Each new area should introduce:
- a new object pool
- higher comprehension requirements
- a stronger visual fantasy
- at least one new aspiration or progression wrinkle when appropriate

The player should feel that their growing IQ lets them access parts of reality they literally could not understand before.

## Area unlock fantasy

Avoid generic copy such as:

```text
World 2
500 IQ
```

Prefer language and presentation around comprehension:

```text
You cannot comprehend this yet.
```

Then, near the threshold:

```text
Something about this structure is beginning to make sense...
```

And at unlock:

```text
You understand.
```

The underlying mechanic can still be a clear IQ threshold. The presentation should make the threshold belong to the game fantasy.

## Starting area — Apple Orchard

The starting floating island combines:
- spawn
- central apple orchard
- six nearby player plots
- Study Station / plot loop
- future Research Center locations
- visible next-area comprehension gate

The orchard is small on purpose. The first area should teach the entire loop without forcing long travel.

See `docs/world/STARTING-ISLAND.md`.

## Within-area progression

An area should ideally contain more than one level of aspiration.

Possible structure:

```text
easy item the player can use immediately
→ medium target visible nearby
→ difficult target that motivates more IQ
→ area gate / next-area landmark
```

For the first prototype, do not add a large catalog just to satisfy this structure. First make the apple interaction fun and readable.

## Future-area design principles

Later areas should:
- have a distinct identity at a glance
- contain objects appropriate to their theme
- remain compact enough that movement is not the dominant activity
- visually tease content above the player's current level
- avoid becoming a straight line of increasingly expensive doors

Potential thematic escalation can move from familiar environments into increasingly intellectual/technological/strange spaces, but no exact sequence is locked yet.

## Portals vs contiguous world

The current starting-island concept can use a comprehension gate/portal/doorway to reach later areas.

That is acceptable even if popular games use physical roads between zones. The important requirement is that the transition feels intentional and fits the "comprehension" theme.

A portal structure should not become a giant generic fantasy monument. It should be readable, mysterious, and proportional to the area.

## Unlock requirements

Default model:
- IQ is the primary area requirement
- money should not normally be required just to enter an area unless a specific narrative/system reason exists
- special facilities or technologies may require both IQ and money

## Backtracking

Older areas should remain useful enough that returning does not feel pointless, but the game does not need to force constant backtracking.

Possible future reasons to revisit:
- newly comprehensible objects
- shortcuts or secrets revealed at higher IQ
- modified/mutated objects
- collection completion

Do not build these systems until the base progression needs them.

## World readability

From important spawn/entry points, the player should quickly identify:
1. current area's main interactive content
2. their route back to plot/home progression
3. the next aspirational gate or landmark

## Open questions

- How many areas should exist at launch?
- Are all areas separate floating islands, or does the visual language evolve later?
- Does higher IQ reveal hidden paths/content inside old areas?
- Does each area have one signature object or a broader object family?
- Is Area 2 reached by teleport, portal, physical bridge, or another transition?
