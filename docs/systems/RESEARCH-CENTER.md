# Research Center

## Status

**CURRENT DIRECTION, PURPOSE NOT FULLY LOCKED.**

The Research Center is the first major plot expansion after the Study Station. Its physical existence is a strong current idea; the exact upgrade systems inside it should be chosen only after the basic visualization/training loop works.

## Fantasy

The Study Station represents personally training the mind.

The Research Center represents using the player's growing intelligence to understand and build more advanced systems.

Visual progression should feel like:

```text
I have a desk where I study
→ I now have an actual facility dedicated to understanding harder things
```

## Role in progression

The Research Center should provide three benefits even before its final systems are known:

1. **Major money goal** — something worth saving for after early Study Station upgrades.
2. **Visible plot expansion** — a player with a Research Center obviously looks more advanced.
3. **Home for deeper upgrades** — future systems have a thematic place instead of becoming random menu buttons.

## Build model

The Research Center has a predetermined authored location on each plot.

Early state:
- empty foundation / build pad / incomplete structure
- clearly labeled future purchase

After purchase:
- visible construction animation
- compact building/facility appears
- interaction point/panel becomes available

Do not make the first version a massive building that consumes the entire plot.

## Possible system responsibilities

These are candidates, not a requirement to implement all of them.

### Movement research

Unlock smarter traversal:
- bicycle/device
- hoverboard
- shortcuts
- teleport/spatial technology

### Visualization research

Improve:
- visualization difficulty tolerance
- vividness/quality bonuses
- information shown about objects
- ability to handle more complex targets

### Study Station sidegrades

Provide choices that are deeper than simply buying the next linear desk tier.

Examples:
- active training efficiency
- passive/offline support if ever adopted
- bonuses for certain object categories

### Item analysis

If variants/mutations/complexity become real systems, the Research Center could identify/analyze them.

### Area technology

Later gates might require understanding a technology or concept in addition to raw IQ.

Only add this if areas need more depth than an IQ threshold.

## UI / interaction

Unlike routine Study Station upgrades, the Research Center may justify a dedicated panel because it can contain several related decisions.

Still keep hierarchy shallow:

```text
interact with Research Center
→ see a small number of meaningful research/upgrades
→ choose/purchase
```

Avoid a giant skill tree until there are enough meaningful upgrades to require one.

## Currency

Default assumption:
- purchases use money
- some technologies require an IQ threshold

Do **not** add `Research Points` automatically.

A research-specific currency should only exist if it supports gameplay that money + IQ cannot explain cleanly.

## Visual style

Early Research Center should match the plot's Roblox-like style and visibly contrast with the primitive Study Station start.

As progression advances it can become increasingly technological, but it should remain readable and not overwhelm neighboring plots.

## Relationship to Study Station

The Research Center should complement, not replace, the Study Station.

Study Station:
- core IQ training
- obvious linear progression anchor

Research Center:
- deeper technology/support choices
- new capabilities
- movement/visualization/advanced systems

## Unlock timing

Current target: after the player already understands:
- visualization
- spinning/training
- IQ
- money
- at least one Study Station upgrade

Do not introduce it during the first seconds of the tutorial.

The build pad can be visible immediately as aspiration, but the system itself should unlock after the core loop is understood.

## Open questions

- What is the first Research Center upgrade?
- Does it unlock movement first, visualization improvements first, or another feature?
- Is the facility upgraded physically through tiers?
- Does it contain one panel or several physical stations?
- Should some research alter old areas/reveal new shortcuts?

## First-pass acceptance criteria

When the first version is eventually implemented:
- build location is obvious before purchase
- Research Center is a meaningful money goal
- construction visibly changes the plot
- at least one useful system lives there
- it does not create a redundant third progression loop
- it does not require a new currency without a clear reason
