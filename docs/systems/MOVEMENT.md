# Movement / Traversal

## Status

**CURRENT DIRECTION / OPEN IMPLEMENTATION.** The problem is understood: repeated travel must not become annoying as the game grows. The exact movement system is not locked.

## Design goal

Movement progression should solve traversal friction while still fitting the intelligence fantasy.

The game should not simply say:

> You have 500 IQ, therefore you run 3× faster.

That is mechanically useful but thematically weak.

Prefer the fantasy:

> You are intelligent enough to understand, build, or perceive a smarter way to travel.

## Early game

The starting island should be compact enough that default Roblox walking is acceptable.

This is why Area 1 is currently targeted around 320–350 studs rather than a huge simulator map.

Do not create a movement problem before the player has movement progression.

## Possible progression ladder

Illustrative only:

```text
normal walking
→ simple practical mobility (bike / skates / equivalent)
→ faster advanced mobility (hoverboard / device)
→ map shortcuts the player can now comprehend
→ teleportation / spatial technology
→ late-game conceptual movement / instant travel
```

The exact devices are not locked.

## Comprehension-based shortcuts

A particularly strong thematic direction is allowing IQ to change how the player perceives old environments.

Examples:
- a route/bridge becomes understandable at higher IQ
- a machine can be operated only after a threshold
- spatial shortcuts reveal themselves
- previously meaningless structures become functional teleport points

This makes IQ feel like it changes the world instead of only changing stats.

## Purchase model

Movement technology may require:
- IQ to comprehend/use it
- money to purchase/build it

This creates a natural two-resource gate when appropriate.

Example:

```text
Hoverboard
Requires 300 IQ
$25,000
```

Numbers are placeholders only.

## Relationship to area size

Do not use future movement upgrades as an excuse to make every area enormous.

Rule of thumb:
- early areas: compact
- later areas: may become broader only when the player already has faster/smarter traversal
- important repeated routes should shorten over time

## Fast travel

Late-game or multi-area progression will likely need fast travel.

Possible forms:
- comprehension portals between discovered areas
- a plot Research Center map/device
- direct teleport to previously understood landmarks

Fast travel should unlock after the player has physically experienced an area at least once unless testing shows otherwise.

## Feel

Movement upgrades should feel like a reward, not only a number change.

Prioritize:
- new animation or silhouette
- distinct speed/handling
- clear unlock moment
- technology visibly matching progression tier

## Avoid

- huge empty paths designed around future speed boosts
- extreme walk-speed inflation that breaks level design
- movement gear that visually clashes with the game's style
- dozens of tiny speed upgrades with no meaningful feel difference

## Open questions

- What is the first movement upgrade?
- Does mobility live on the plot, Research Center, or as direct purchases?
- Does the player own one active vehicle/device at a time?
- How early should fast travel appear?
- Are higher-IQ shortcuts a major feature or occasional surprises?
