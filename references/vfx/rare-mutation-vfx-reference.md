# Rare / Mutation VFX Reference

## Reference purpose

This video is a strong reference for Roblox IQ's high-intensity VFX for:

- mutations
- very rare Concepts
- special variants
- rare spawns
- major reward moments

The useful part is not the exact source game theme.

The important idea is:

> rare or mutated things should look immediately exciting and visually overloaded in a satisfying way.

This should create strong dopamine moments.

---

# What I like

This reference makes rare things feel special through very visible effects.

Important traits:

- strong glow
- bright particle bursts
- layered energy effects
- large readable aura around rare things
- obvious visual intensity
- effects are impossible to ignore
- rarity feels visible, not just written in text

The overall feeling is:

> "This thing is powerful / mutated / rare."

That is exactly the kind of communication Roblox IQ should aim for.

---
