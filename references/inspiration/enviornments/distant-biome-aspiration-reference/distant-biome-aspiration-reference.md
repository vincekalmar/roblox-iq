# Distant Biome Aspiration Reference

## Reference purpose

This screenshot is a strong reference for Roblox IQ's world progression and area presentation.

The most important idea is:

> Future areas are visible before the player can access them.

The player can look far down the world and see increasingly strange, impressive, or advanced biomes.

This creates curiosity and aspiration before the player understands exactly what those areas contain.

The goal is not to copy the long straight corridor layout.

The useful design principle is making future progression physically visible.

---

# What I like

The player can see much farther into the progression path than their current area.

Even from an early area, distant content is visible.

This creates questions like:

- "What is that?"
- "How do I get there?"
- "What kind of objects are in that area?"
- "How much stronger do I need to become?"
- "Why does that area look so crazy?"

The world itself becomes an advertisement for future progression.

This is much stronger than hiding every future area behind menus or completely opaque teleport buttons.

# Roblox IQ adaptation

Roblox IQ currently uses a floating-island world concept.

Instead of placing every biome along one long physical corridor, future areas could exist as separate floating islands visible in the distance.
